01 / Regulatory case management

Irish competition &
consumer-protection regulator

Solution architecture & development

  • D365 Customer Service
  • Dataverse
  • Power Pages
  • Azure Functions
  • PCF
  • Azure DevOps
Context
An organisation-wide Dynamics 365 Customer Service and Dataverse case-management platform, built from the ground up across two phases.
Challenge
Support the complete case lifecycle: intake, assessment, investigation, evidence, correspondence, approvals, enforcement and closure — with secure external submissions and document governance.
Architecture / what I built
Co-authored the Solution Design and led three junior developers. Built Power Pages evidence submission with Azure Functions, Custom APIs/plugins, SharePoint and Application Insights; a configurable cross-environment sync service; and PCF controls for document upload, approval visibility and Eircode/address lookup. Developed an offline-first inspection PWA with Dataverse sync, local persistence, recoverable outbox and retry handling. Governed Azure DevOps ALM using managed solutions.
Outcome
End-to-end case management, recoverable offline inspections and controlled promotion across environments. Strengthened privacy through DSAR anonymisation and least-privilege security design.
02 / National healthcare recruitment

Irish national
healthcare organisation

Solution architect & technical lead

  • D365 CE
  • Power Pages
  • Power Automate
  • SharePoint
  • Security architecture
Context
A national healthcare recruitment platform serving approximately 3,000 users, transitioning from a legacy structure to a hierarchical regional operating model.
Challenge
Introduce regional operations while preserving controlled visibility of legacy job orders and amendments across a connected multi-environment estate.
Architecture / what I built
Designed business units, owner teams, roles and record ownership. Shaped centrally maintained approver/submitter configuration and portal routing. Coordinated multiple D365 and Power Pages environments, SharePoint and 20+ Power Automate flows. Defined DEV/UAT isolation, parallel support releases and legacy-data coexistence.
Outcome
Replaced fragile manual selection with governed reference data, dynamic regional filtering and permission-aware approval behaviour, while preserving controlled access to legacy records.
